Who We Are

Welcome to Elevate Home Renovation, your trusted partner for premium home construction and restoration services in Ireland. We are dedicated to turning your living spaces into beautiful, functional, and durable homes.

Whether you want to build your custom dream home from scratch, extend your living space, or completely remodel your property, our team delivers outstanding results with unmatched attention to detail.

Our History: A Journey of Excellence (Since 2000)

Our story began in Ukraine in the year 2000, founded on a simple principle: to build with integrity and craft with passion. For over two decades, we established a solid reputation as a premier contractor, completing hundreds of successful residential projects.

In 2022, we expanded our horizons and brought our rich legacy and deep expertise to Ireland. Today, we proudly combine our 26 years of European construction experience with local Irish building standards to deliver modern, safe, and beautifully restored homes that stand the test of time.
